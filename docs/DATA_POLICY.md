# Public data policy

This portfolio repository follows a code-open, vendor-data-closed policy.

Included:

- source code and tests;
- frozen model bundles and their hashes;
- synthetic examples;
- aggregate forecast metrics;
- derived strategy equity, annual results, and a sanitized trade summary;
- screenshots and research documentation.

Excluded:

- API credentials and local environment files;
- raw or normalized TqSdk, Databento, OPRA, and exchange records;
- raw bid/ask, top-of-book, and tick histories;
- data caches and internal package archives.

The inclusion of a downloader does not grant permission to download or
redistribute third-party data. Users must obtain their own subscriptions and
comply with every provider's current terms.
